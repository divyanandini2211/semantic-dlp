NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)
========================================

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of April 20, 2026
("Effective Date") by and between:

  Disclosing Party:
    Name:           Meridian Capital Partners LLC
    Address:        350 Park Avenue, 21st Floor, New York, NY 10022
    Represented by: Rachel Huang, General Counsel

  Receiving Party:
    Name:           Quantum Ventures Group Inc.
    Address:        1 Market Plaza, Suite 1800, San Francisco, CA 94105
    Represented by: Thomas Bryce, CEO

----------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
----------------------------------------

The parties intend to explore a potential strategic partnership or investment
opportunity in connection with Project Falcon (the "Transaction"). In connection
with this evaluation, Meridian Capital Partners LLC may disclose certain
Confidential Information to Quantum Ventures Group Inc.

----------------------------------------
2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
----------------------------------------

"Confidential Information" means any and all technical, financial, business,
legal, or operational information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, including
but not limited to:
  - Financial performance data, projections, and deal terms
  - Client lists, vendor relationships, and pricing agreements
  - Proprietary algorithms, source code, and technical architectures
  - Employee compensation details and personnel records
  - Merger and acquisition plans, target company identities, and deal structures

----------------------------------------
3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY
----------------------------------------

The Receiving Party agrees to:
  a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence
  b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior
     written consent of the Disclosing Party
  c) Use Confidential Information solely for evaluation of the Transaction
  d) Notify Disclosing Party immediately upon discovering any unauthorized
     disclosure or breach of this Agreement
  e) Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon written request

----------------------------------------
4. TERM AND TERMINATION
----------------------------------------

This Agreement shall remain in effect for three (3) years from the Effective
Date, unless earlier terminated by mutual written consent.

Survival: Sections 2, 3, 5, and 6 shall survive termination.

----------------------------------------
5. REMEDIES
----------------------------------------

The Receiving Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause
irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy.
Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief
and other equitable remedies without bond or other security.

Liquidated damages for material breach: USD 2,500,000 per incident.

----------------------------------------
6. GOVERNING LAW
----------------------------------------

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
Dispute resolution: binding arbitration under JAMS rules (New York venue).
